Interpretation: kernfs serializes callbacks that use the same kernfs_open_file. Separate opens have separate of->mutex instances; this lock does not serialize awrw_show and awrw_store issued through different open file descriptions. The driver callbacks have no shared lock around the pointer replacement/free and show dereference, so the stock lifetime race remains possible.
